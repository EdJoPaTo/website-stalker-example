It's a real computer
----------

Intel® Core™ Series 3 delivers up to 70% longer battery life[ϟ](#fw12-battery-info-anchor) streaming Netflix 4K than the previous generation, all while running cool and quiet. Packed into a 12.2" convertible body, it brings the full power of a lightweight Intel processor alongside dual Thunderbolt™ 4 ports for high-speed peripherals, 4K displays, and external GPU expansion.

##### Display #####

12.2" touchscreen, 1920x1200 resolution, \>400nit brightness for outdoor use, with USI 2.0 stylus support.

##### CPU #####

Intel® Core™ Series 3 processors up to 4.8GHz, with 2P + 4 LPE cores, and Intel® Graphics.

##### Memory #####

Up to 64GB of high-speed upgradeable DDR5 with a single SO-DIMM slot.

##### Storage #####

Up to 2TB M.2 2230 NVMe™ SSDs, easy to swap when you need more space.

##### I/O #####

4 slots for the Framework Expansion Card system, including 2x Thunderbolt 4 ports and a 3.5mm audio jack.

##### Wi-Fi #####

Supports Wi-Fi 7 Multi-Link Operation, including 2.4GHz, 5GHz, and 6GHz network bands, with an upgradeable Intel® BE213 card.

One laptop, every angle
----------

Framework Laptop 12 is a true convertible. The 360-degree hinge moves through three modes: clamshell for daily work, tent for watching and presenting (or as a second screen when docked), and tablet for sketching and marking up directly on the 12.2" touchscreen. Touch input and USI 2.0 stylus support mean you can work the way you want, in any mode.

Pick your ports
----------

External adapters are a thing of the past. Framework's Expansion Card system lets you choose exactly which ports you have, and exactly where they sit on the laptop. Mix and match USB-C, USB-A, HDMI, DisplayPort, and storage cards to connect older peripherals, add storage on the fly, or drive up to two 4K displays at 60Hz, two per Thunderbolt 4 port over DisplayPort 2.1.

Privacy
----------

Your privacy is protected at a hardware level, with physical switches that electrically cut off the webcam and microphones whenever you need.

Keyboard
----------

Responsive full-size keys with 1.5mm key travel. Made in a wide range of language and layout options, now including an optional backlit US English Keyboard.

Fingerprint reader
----------

Instantly sign in with the optional fingerprint reader. It works with Windows Hello on Windows 11 and with libfprint 1.94.100 or newer on Linux. Available now on some distros with more coming soon. And because it's built into the replaceable Power Button Board, you can add it whenever you want, or skip it entirely.
