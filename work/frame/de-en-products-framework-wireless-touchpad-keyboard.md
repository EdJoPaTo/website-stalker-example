How do pre-orders work?
----------

A pre-order reserves your spot in a production batch. When you place your pre-order, we charge a deposit. The remaining balance, including applicable taxes and shipping, is charged shortly before your order ships. You can cancel for a full refund of your deposit any time before the remaining balance is charged.

How much is the deposit?
----------

The deposit amount is shown on the product page and at checkout before you place your pre-order.

FAQs
----------

* What happens after I place my pre-order?

  1. **Pre-order confirmed.** We charge your deposit and email you your order number and shipping window. You can view your order anytime in your Framework account, and you can cancel for a full refund until your remaining balance is charged.
  2. **“We’re preparing your order” email.** This email means your remaining balance will be charged within 5 days. Check your shipping address and payment method by the date in the email.
  3. **Payment complete.** We email you once the remaining balance is charged. Your order can no longer be canceled. It goes to our warehouse and ships within 5 business days.
  4. **Your order has shipped.** We email you a tracking number. Most orders arrive within 5 business days of shipping.

  If your shipping window changes, we’ll email you and update the order summary in your Framework account.

  Unfortunately, pre-orders and in-stock items can’t be purchased in the same order. To buy in-stock items, place a separate order and they’ll ship on their own.

* When will my pre-order ship?

  Your pre-order ships within the window shown for your batch when you ordered. You can see it anytime in your Framework account under Orders. When we have a more specific date, we’ll email you and update it there.

* Why does my pre-order show a quarter instead of a specific month?

  Some products ship in larger batches, so each batch covers a wider shipping window, such as Q1 2027 (January, February, or March). We’ll narrow it down to a specific date as production progresses and we have better visibility on shipment dates.

* When will I be charged?

  Your deposit is charged when you place your pre-order. The remaining balance, including applicable taxes and shipping, is charged to your saved payment method within 5 days of receiving the “We’re preparing your order” email. The exact amount is listed in that email.

* Can I cancel my pre-order?

  Yes, until your remaining balance is charged. Cancel from your Framework account under Orders, and your deposit is refunded to your original payment method. Once the remaining balance is charged, your order is being prepared for shipment and can’t be canceled. You can still return it after delivery under our [standard return policy](https://knowledgebase.frame.work/what-is-the-framework-return-policy-Byhrd6uud).

* Can I change my shipping address or payment method?

  Yes. Update them in your Framework account by the deadline in your “We’re preparing your order” email. If you change your shipping address, taxes and shipping may change your remaining balance. Please check the updated total before you confirm.

* What if my payment for the remaining balance doesn’t go through?

  Make sure your saved payment method is valid and has enough available credit before the deadline in your “We’re preparing your order” email. If we can’t charge it, we’ll email you and try again over the next 2 weeks. If the payment still doesn’t go through after 3 attempts, your order will be canceled and we’ll email you to let you know.

* Can I add in-stock items to my pre-order?

  Unfortunately, pre-orders and in-stock items can’t be purchased in the same order. To buy in-stock items, place a separate order and they’ll ship on their own.

* What if my pre-order is delayed?

  If your shipping window changes, we’ll email you with the new timing and update your Framework account. You can cancel for a full refund of your deposit any time before your remaining balance is charged.

* Will my pre-order arrive before the holidays?

  No. Pre-orders are scheduled to ship in Q1 2027, so they won’t arrive before the December holidays. If you’re ordering it as a gift, plan for it to arrive in the new year.

Works with (almost) everything you have
----------

The Framework Wireless Touchpad Keyboard works with your TV setup or HTPC, with Linux and Windows on your Framework Laptop, Framework Desktop, mini PC, home server, or Raspberry Pi, and even with your Mac in Mac Mode! It also pairs with tablets and phones, and for gaming it works with PS5, Xbox, docked handheld PCs, sim rigs, and the Steam Machine.

Built for where you use it
----------

**Living room**

Run your HTPC from the couch, whether you’re searching your library, signing in, or handling the pop-ups your controller can’t. With more than 10m of wireless range, it easily reaches your TV from across the room.

**Workbench**

Connect it to a headless server, mini PC, or Raspberry Pi, and it’s ready from power-on, so BIOS setup doesn’t mean hunting for a spare keyboard.

**Desk**

Use one keyboard and touchpad for your work laptop, tablet, and gaming PC, and switch between them with a single button press.

**Sim rig**

Handle setup, chat, and menus from your seat without reaching for a mouse, and download the open CAD files from [GitHub](https://github.com/FrameworkComputer/Framework-Wireless-Touchpad-Keyboard) to design or 3D print a mount that fits your cockpit.

Sized for your setup
----------

The keyboard and touchpad share one compact chassis, so it sits comfortably on your lap, a sim rig tray, or a narrow desk shelf. It stays rigid wherever you put it, and the profile tapers toward the front edge for a natural typing angle.

Made to last
----------

We designed this keyboard the same way we design our laptops, with parts you can replace and firmware you can keep updating. The battery is rechargeable, and if you need a new one, you can pick it up in the Framework Marketplace, along with a new USB-A Dongle if you lose yours. Keeping the firmware up to date is just as easy. Updates install from a Framework web page in Chrome or Edge with just one click.

Made to be yours
----------

### ZMK Firmware ###

Remap keys with [ZMK Studio](https://zmk.studio/) in your browser, with no reflashing required. When you want to go deeper, the firmware is an open-source fork of ZMK, so you can edit the config directly and change anything the tool doesn’t cover.

### Control Board ###

The Control Board is sold on its own, built around a Nordic nRF54LM20A with 28 GPIO pins on a socketable 0.1" header. Its schematic, pinout, and bootloader will be published on GitHub, ready for you to make it the brain of your own custom keyboard.

[Learn more](/products/framework-wireless-touchpad-keyboard-control-board)
