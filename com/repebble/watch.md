Know what you’re getting into
----------

### What Pebble is ###

* **Weeks per charge**Up to 30 days on Time 2, about 2 weeks on Round 2.
* **Made to glance at**Glance at a notification, then get back to your day.
* **Real buttons**Four buttons you can use without looking, plus touch.
* **Yours to tinker with**Open-source PebbleOS and 18,000+ watchfaces and apps.

### What Pebble isn’t ###

* ~~A triathlon watch~~It counts steps and sleep (Time 2 adds heart rate), but get a Garmin if you want serious workout tracking.
* ~~An Apple Watch replacement~~Pebble is simpler, and doesn’t have every feature (like payments). Apple also restricts 3rd party smartwatches in some ways.

You shouldn’t get a Pebble if you require a perfectly polished smartwatch. This era of Pebble is a labour of love rather than a startup trying to sell millions of watches. There may be some rough edges (literally). It could break. It might not last as long as you’d like. What we can guarantee is that it will be awesome and tons of fun! Every time you look down at your watch, you will smile 🙂

– Eric (Pebble Founder)

FAQ
----------

### Do they track health? ###

Both track steps and sleep. Time 2 tracks heart rate and SpO2. Neither has a GPS.

### Does it work with Index 01? ###

Yes. Answers and notifications from Index 01 can show up on your wrist.

### When will it ship? ###

Time 2 is in stock and ships within 48 hours by tracked courier. Round 2 is a pre-order, and we’ll email you before it ships.

You can cancel Round 2 pre-order and get a full refund (minus credit card transaction fees) any time before your watch ships.

### Will I pay duties or import fees? ###

Taxes and duties are calculated at checkout. US orders include a US import tariff of $3.31 per Pebble Time 2 or $3.60 per Pebble Round 2, shown at checkout. All shipments are sent from our warehouse in Asia via courier with tracking. This means that non-US shipments will not be subjected to US tariffs. Your country may have its own taxes, tariffs, customs or duties fees. Shipments are sent ‘DDP’ which means that all taxes, tariffs and duties are prepaid.

### Can I change straps? ###

Yes. Each watch comes with a quick release strap. Time 2 takes standard 22 mm straps. Round 2 comes in two variants: 20mm and 14mm straps.

You can swap the strap for any standard quick release watch strap.

### Is there a warranty? ###

### Is PebbleOS really open source? ###

Yes. PebbleOS and the mobile app are open source, and you can modify PebbleOS and run your changes on your own watch.

### Can I make my own watchface? ###

Yes. The [SDK](https://developer.repebble.com/sdk) is free, and plenty of owners build their first watchface in a weekend on [CloudPebble](https://cloudpebble.repebble.com/).
