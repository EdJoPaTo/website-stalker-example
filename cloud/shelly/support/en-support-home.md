Black Friday 2026

Quick Support FAQ – Please Check Before Opening a Ticket
----------

Due to the exceptionally high volume of orders and customer inquiries during Black Friday, response times may be longer than usual. The information below answers the most common campaign-related questions.

**⚠ Black Friday Campaign:** **19 November 2026, 09:00 EET – 2 December 2026, 23:59 EET.** Discounts of up to **60%** apply to selected products while stocks last.

**1️⃣ Delivery Times & Tracking**

Courier and logistics networks experience significantly higher parcel volumes during Black Friday. Delivery may therefore take longer than the standard estimates.

* Once your order is shipped, a tracking link will be sent by email.
* Courier tracking updates may occasionally be delayed by several business days.
* Shelly Support cannot accelerate a parcel once it has been handed over to the courier.
* If there have been **no tracking updates for more than 14 days**, please contact us so we can investigate with the courier.

[➡ View Delivery & Returns information](https://www.shelly.com/pages/delivery-returns)

**? Black Friday Free Shipping**

Free standard shipping is automatically applied when the product value after discounts reaches:

* **€150:** Austria, Belgium, Croatia, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia and Sweden.
* **€79:** Germany, Italy and Spain.
* **€50:** Bulgaria.
* **PLN 330:** Poland.
* **US Store:** free standard shipping from **$50** within the United States.

Thresholds are calculated after discounts and exclude shipping fees. EU order value includes VAT; US order value excludes sales tax.

**2️⃣ Order Status**

* You will receive an email confirmation after your order has been successfully placed.
* A second email will be sent once your order has been shipped.
* You can check the current status at any time from your Shelly account under **My Orders**.

**3️⃣ Invoices**

* Invoices are generated once the order is shipped.
* The invoice can be downloaded from the shipping confirmation email: **“Order XXXXXXX shipped — find your invoice below.”**
* Please check both your inbox and spam folder before contacting Support.

**4️⃣ Order Modifications**

* Your **shipping address can be edited within 15 minutes** after placing the order from the Thank You / Order Status page.
* After this period, changes can no longer be made.
* Products cannot be edited or added, and separate orders cannot be merged.

**5️⃣ Out-of-Stock Products**

* Select **“Notify when back in stock”** on the product page to receive an automatic email notification.
* Due to rapidly changing Black Friday demand, Support cannot provide guaranteed restock dates or reserve out-of-stock products.

**6️⃣ Vouchers & Discount Codes**

During the Black Friday campaign, **regular voucher and discount codes are disabled and cannot be used.**

**✓ Replacement codes will continue working.** Codes issued specifically for defective product replacements remain valid during the Black Friday campaign.

**★ Free Gifts**

* **EU orders from €200:** free Shelly Smart Water Bottle.
* **EU orders from €400:** free Shelly multimeter and Shelly Smart Water Bottle.
* **US orders from $100:** free Shelly multitool.
* The gift must be added to the cart through the campaign pop-up before completing the order.
* Gifts are available while stocks last and are limited to one gift set per order.

If a return reduces the order below the required qualifying value, the gift must also be returned or its regular price may be deducted from the refund.

VIP List **★ Shelly VIP List Benefits**

* VIP List customers automatically receive **Protection Plus extended warranty** free of charge for eligible products ordered using their VIP List email address.
* Existing VIP List members automatically receive the available Black Friday VIP perks.
* For the VIP Giveaway, the qualifying order must be placed using the **same email address used for the VIP List**.
* Eligible VIP participants may enter the Black Friday Prize Draw and can win **store credit equal to their qualifying order value, up to €200**.
* VIP Giveaway eligibility and country restrictions are governed by the separate VIP Campaign Terms.

[➡ Black Friday / VIP Campaign information](https://www.shelly.com/pages/black-friday)

**! Important Campaign Conditions**

* Discounts differ by product, country and store and are valid while stocks last.
* Campaign discounts do not apply to tax rates, import or customs duties..
* Free shipping and gift thresholds are calculated using the product value **after discounts**.
* Regular voucher and discount codes are **disabled during the Black Friday campaign**.
* **Replacement codes issued for defective products remain active and can still be used.**
* Consumer statutory rights remain unaffected.

**Before submitting a ticket:** Please review the information above and check your order confirmation, shipping email and customer account first. You may find the answer immediately without waiting for a Support response. **Thank you for your patience and cooperation during Black Friday.**
